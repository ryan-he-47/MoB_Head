## Top-5 highest-leverage recommendations for THIS architecture

1. Per-component post-hoc temperature scaling on bit logits (S2; cost: hours). Fit one scalar T_k per component on dev NLL after training. Round decode reads each component standalone, but training only optimized the MIXTURE; nothing calibrated individual components. Cheapest plausible chunk of the 0.770 -> 0.798 gap. Basis: Guo et al. 2017.
2. Replace constant pi-entropy regularizer with a two-phase pi-pressure schedule (S1). Phase A (early): utilization pressure - Switch aux loss or auxiliary-loss-free bias (Wang et al. 2024, keeps distillation NLL clean). Phase B (late): pruning pressure - sparse-Dirichlet-style +eps*sum(log pi) (Rousseau-Mengersen alpha<d/2; Figueiredo-Jain annihilation), then SMEM-merge surviving vertex-sharing clones. Rationale: entropy bonus = Dirichlet(alpha>1) = the keeping regime; it mathematically cannot empty the 30% clones. If the user WANTS 128 utilized components, keep phase A only - but pick one goal; one knob cannot do both.
3. Evolving-WTA / per-component auxiliary responsibility loss (S2 + S1; structural fix). Makansi EWTA adapted to distillation: each position's top-responsibility component(s) get an additional direct per-component loss (annealed weight), so components become calibrated modes, not mixture shards. The literature's canonical answer to "mixture fine, components meaningless".
4. Extend temperature annealing to bit logits (S6 + sigma-saturation gradients). DAEM's original tempering covers the whole responsibility (pi AND component density); softening only pi while components stay razor-sharp is an E/M mismatch. Temper per-bit sigmoids (or smooth teacher bit-labels) early -> gradients keep flowing to losing components' bits, countering rich-get-richer. Gumbel/Concrete practice supplies schedule priors (exponential; too fast hurts). One-flag ablation.
5. Uniform background component with learnable weight (S3/tail; cost: hours). Component K+1 with bit-logits pinned at 0 (uniform over vertices). Banfield-Raftery: absorbs outliers so real components stay clean; Jelinek-Mercer: this IS interpolated smoothing. Its learned weight is a free diffuseness diagnostic; watch it doesn't grow to hide model deficiency.

## Known traps (literature says NOT to do)

- T1. Fixed entropy-reg lambda asked to do two jobs: entropy bonus = Dirichlet(alpha>1) prior -> uniform pi -> KEEPS clones (opposite of pruning regime). lambda sensitivity documented (Pereyra 2017). Decide utilization OR pruning; schedule the switch.
- T2. One-shot annealing without structural follow-through: collapse returns once KL annealing ends (Lucas et al. 2019). Expect pi-clone re-formation after DAEM unless phase-B pressure/merging follows. Cyclical annealing (Fu et al. 2019) is a cheap alternative.
- T3. Annealing too fast: too-fast temperature decay measurably degrades final quality (HQ-VAE sweep); DA theory requires slow cooling through phase transitions (Rose 1998). 10%-of-steps DAEM window is aggressive; sweep it when running the S6 ablation.
- T4. Hard/WTA assignment from step 0: starves losers; their saturated sigmoids receive ~zero bit-gradient (the rich-get-richer pathology). EWTA exists precisely to fix this; if used, anneal k, don't hard-assign at birth.
- T5. Annihilating many components at once: Figueiredo-Jain kill one at a time and re-normalize; mass pruning mid-run shocks the remaining mixture. Same care for RPCL rival de-learning strength.
- T6. Label/beta-smoothing the TEACHER distribution: under distillation that is a target change, not a regularizer - biases the student away from the teacher's true top-1024 distribution. Pereyra's smoothing/confidence equivalence applies to one-hot targets, not soft teacher targets.
- T7. Reading n_eff as ground-truth mode count: ICL underestimates K under overlap; BIC overestimates under misspecification. Held-out NLL elbow is primary; criteria are secondary.
- T8. Post-hoc per-component temperature cannot create information: it re-scales saturated logits and fixes ranking-under-rounding, not the mixture NLL. Report both mixture-level and component-level metrics after applying it (calibration can mask, not cure, S2).
- T9. Background weight unmonitored: it can quietly absorb mass that semantic components should own (garbage-collector failure). Cap/monitor pi_bg; growth = model-deficiency alarm.
- T10. Zero/identical component init without a symmetry breaker (already avoided via k-means-vertex init - keep it; Shazeer zero-init gate works only because the noise term breaks symmetry).
